## 1. Executive Summary

- Bob's Watches executed a significant inventory restructuring this week, adding 3,343 new URLs while removing 3,340. The net change is nearly flat, but the composition shift matters: they appear to be retiring legacy "new-model" URL formats (e.g., `new-model-rolex-datejust-116200.html`, `new-rolex-gmt-master-ii-116713.html`) in favor of standardized `pre-owned-` and `used-` slug conventions, likely a technical SEO cleanup to consolidate crawl equity and reduce duplicate content.
- The most strategically notable expansion is Breitling. Bob's added over 40 new Breitling product pages this week (Navitimer, Superocean, Avenger, Chronomat, Endurance Pro, Top Time, Premier, Windrider) and updated another 40-plus existing ones. This is a deliberate push to own pre-owned Breitling search demand.
- Bob's also expanded into Cartier (Tank, Santos, Panthere, Pasha, Roadster) and Grand Seiko with fresh inventory pages, signaling a broader multi-brand pre-owned strategy beyond their Rolex core.
- Wrist Aficionado does not carry Breitling and has no Breitling content. This is a clean gap to monitor, but the more urgent action is to protect existing turf by strengthening high-intent pages where Bob's is actively updating inventory for competing models.

Wrist Aficionado already has `/collections/panthere-de-cartier`. Bob's updated and added Panthere pages this week. Make sure this page is competitive in content depth, especially for queries around Panthere medium model and diamond bezel variants.

**4. Build a Breitling brand hub.**

If Wrist Aficionado carries or plans to carry Breitling, this is the moment to build a `/collections/breitling` landing page and at minimum a Navitimer and Superocean collection page. If the page is thin, add structured content covering the key collections.

**6. Create a Vintage Rolex Bubbleback reference guide.**
Bob's added five Oyster Perpetual Ref. 3131 Bubbleback pages this week, including multiple slug variants, suggesting this reference is getting intentional SEO attention. Wrist Aficionado has `/collections/antique-and-vintage` and `/collections/vintage-watches` but no dedicated Bubbleback content. If Wrist Aficionado carries or sources vintage Rolex, a focused editorial piece on the Bubbleback (history, reference numbers, what to look for, current market) would capture collector-intent traffic that Bob's is targeting through inventory pages alone. Confirm inventory availability before building.

**7. Monitor Bob's URL migration for ranking volatility.**
Bob's removed over 3,300 URLs this week, mostly legacy `new-model-` and `new-rolex-` Rolex pages. Large-scale URL removals and redirects frequently cause temporary ranking drops for the migrating site. Watch Bob's Rolex-related rankings in the coming two to three weeks for softness. If rankings dip on terms like "pre-owned Rolex Datejust" or "used Rolex GMT Master II," be prepared to push budget toward those terms in paid or accelerate content updates to Wrist Aficionado's existing Rolex Datejust and GMT pages to capture displaced traffic.
