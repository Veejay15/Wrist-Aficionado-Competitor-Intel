The dofollow/nofollow split is heavily skewed toward dofollow, with 795,892 dofollow links against 32,304 nofollow links (roughly 96% dofollow). The top anchor by raw backlink count is "Bobs Watches" with 193,191 links, followed by a large block of image/empty anchors. A significant concern is visible in the anchor data: multiple high-volume spam anchors referencing Telegram-based link-selling services (e.g., "TELEGRAM @SEO_ANOMALY" with 6,202 links across 300 referring domains, and "TG @BHS_LINKS" with 5,710 links) indicate an ongoing negative SEO or link spam problem that could draw algorithmic scrutiny. The most-linked page is the homepage with 246,729 backlinks, followed by the /sell-rolex-watch page at 3,836 backlinks, which reflects Bob's commercial positioning around the Rolex resale funnel.

All 40 new backlinks recorded in the period originated from a single domain, notjustformen.com, which carries a domain inlink rank of 29 on every linking page.
