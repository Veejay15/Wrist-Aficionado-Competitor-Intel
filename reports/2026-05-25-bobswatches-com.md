## 5. Recommended Actions for Wrist Aficionado

**1. Strengthen the existing Vacheron Constantin collection pages, do not build new ones.**
Wrist Aficionado already has `/collections/vacheron-constantin`, `/collections/vacheron-constantin-overseas`, `/collections/vacheron-constantin-fiftysix`, `/collections/vacheron-constantin-traditionnelle`, and `/collections/vacheron-constantin-historiques-222`. Bob's just rebuilt their VC architecture from a single flat page into a structured brand hub. Respond by adding editorial depth to the existing VC collection pages. Each page should include a model overview paragraph, key reference numbers carried, and an internal link to any relevant blog content. The goal is to match or exceed the informational depth Bob's is building into their new model sub-pages.

**2. Prioritize content to defend the "royal oak" keyword position before Bob's consolidates.**
Bob's just jumped to position 6 on "royal oak" (74,000 volume, commercial intent). Wrist Aficionado has strong AP Royal Oak inventory and multiple collection pages including `/collections/audemars-piguet-royal-oak` and `/collections/audemars-piguet-royal-oak-jumbo`. Review the on-page optimization of the primary Royal Oak collection page. Ensure it has a clear H1 targeting "Audemars Piguet Royal Oak," a substantive editorial intro (150 to 200 words minimum), and internal links to the existing blog content at `/blogs/news/the-ultimate-audemars-piguet-royal-oak-jumbo-buyer-s-guide-in-2025` and `/blogs/news/top-10-audemars-piguet-royal-oak-watches-for-collectors-in-2025`. This week's ranking jump is a direct competitive signal.

**3. Build a "Patek Philippe vs. Rolex" editorial piece.**
Bob's editorial at `/rolex-blog/editorial/patek-philippe-vs-rolex.html` is capturing PAA position 2 for "patek philippe" (246,000 searches/month) and "patek phillippe" (135,000 searches/month). Wrist Aficionado does not have an equivalent comparison piece. This is a confirmed content gap. A well-structured comparison article covering heritage, investment value, resale performance, and collector positioning would compete directly for these PAA placements, which Bob's is owning with low competition from other resellers.

**4. Commission a "Why are Rolex Watches so Expensive" editorial or a "What Affects Luxury Watch Resale Value" piece.**
Both URLs are receiving inbound links from topically relevant sites this week (rkwatchservice.com). More importantly, these are the types of evergreen, high-intent informational pieces that accumulate links passively over time. Wrist Aficionado has strong resale and investment content (e.g., `/blogs/news/daily-wear-vs-investment-patek-philippe-aquanaut-travel-time`, `/blogs/news/what-collectors-should-do-when-a-rolex-or-patek-is-discontinued`) but lacks a direct "why Rolex holds value" or "luxury watch resale factors" explainer. This fills a topical authority gap and attracts the same editorial linking pattern Bob's is benefiting from.

**5. Target the PGA Tour / golf audience with a curated watch collection or editorial.**
Bob's secured press coverage this week from a collaboration with PGA Tour golfer Patrick Rodgers (lincolncitizen.com, DR 46). This is a brand-building and link-earning play that Wrist Aficionado could replicate with its own athlete or sports-adjacent celebrity angle. Wrist Aficionado already has strong athlete watch collection pages (Patrick Mahomes, LeBron James, Stephen Curry, etc.). A "watches of the PGA Tour" or "golf watches for collectors" curated editorial would be a natural fit and a direct counter to the audience segment Bob's is courting with the Rodgers partnership.

**6. Monitor Bob's Cartier expansion but do not react yet.**
Bob's added 10+ new Cartier Tank and Santos SKUs this week and simultaneously dropped 9 positions on "cartier watch." The Cartier category expansion is real but not yet producing keyword results. Wrist Aficionado carries Cartier inventory with collection pages at `/collections/cartier`, `/collections/cartier-tank`, `/collections/cartier-santos`, and `/collections/cartier-ballon-bleu`. Hold position and monitor over the next two to three weeks. If Bob's Cartier category page starts recovering position on "cartier watch," escalate the response to include editorial content on Cartier model guides.
