**2. Expand the existing `/collections/vacheron-constantin-overseas` and related VC pages into a deeper brand architecture.**
Bob's updated their `/vacheron-constantin/` hub this week alongside adding the Historiques sub-page. Wrist Aficionado has four VC collection pages (`/collections/vacheron-constantin`, `/collections/vacheron-constantin-fiftysix`, `/collections/vacheron-constantin-historiques-222`, `/collections/vacheron-constantin-overseas`, `/collections/vacheron-constantin-traditionnelle`).
