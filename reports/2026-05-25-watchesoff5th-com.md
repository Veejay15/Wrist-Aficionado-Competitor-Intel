**1. Publish a Vacheron Constantin editorial piece targeting the "vacheron vs patek" comparison query.**
Watches Off 5th just built a page targeting this directly. Wrist Aficionado has Vacheron collection pages at /collections/vacheron-constantin, /collections/vacheron-constantin-overseas, /collections/vacheron-constantin-traditionnelle, and /collections/vacheron-constantin-fiftysix, but no editorial blog content on Vacheron Constantin exists in the existing page list. A comparison article framed as "Vacheron Constantin vs Patek Philippe: Which Should You Buy?" is a high-intent consideration-stage piece that serves exactly the type of buyer Wrist Aficionado targets. Publish this week before Watches Off 5th's new page earns ranking traction.

**2. Publish a Vacheron Constantin model guide article.**
The URL slug /blogs/news/vacheron-constantin-model-guide does not exist on wristaficionado.com and no equivalent guide exists in the page list. Given the collection infrastructure already built out for Vacheron, an editorial piece covering key references (Overseas, Traditionnelle, FiftySix, Historiques 222) with internal links to those collection pages would strengthen topical authority and support the commercial pages. This is a content gap Watches Off 5th is now moving to own.

**3. Strengthen the Audemars Piguet Royal Oak collection page at /collections/audemars-piguet-royal-oak to compete with Watches Off 5th's gains on "audemars piguet royal oak" and "ap royal oak."**
Watches Off 5th jumped from position 10 to position 4 and from position 5 to position 2 on these two terms using its /collections/royal-oak page. Wrist Aficionado has the equivalent page. Audit the page for content depth, add structured introductory copy covering why the Royal Oak is collectible, reference key references (15500ST, 15202ST Jumbo, 26315ST Offshore), and ensure internal links from the existing Royal Oak buyer's guide at /blogs/news/the-ultimate-audemars-piguet-royal-oak-jumbo-buyer-s-guide-in-2025 and the AP reference number guide at /blogs/news/the-ultimate-guide-to-audemars-piguet-reference-numbers-in-2025 point strongly to this collection page.
