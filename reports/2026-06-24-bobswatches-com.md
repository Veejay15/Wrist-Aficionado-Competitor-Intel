## 1. Executive Summary

- Bob's Watches executed a major sitemap restructuring this week, adding 2,148 new URLs while simultaneously removing 2,147 URLs. - The Breitling catalog received the heaviest indexing push, with over a dozen new and updated listing pages spanning the Navitimer, Superocean, Avenger, Aerospace, Chronomat, and Top Time families.
