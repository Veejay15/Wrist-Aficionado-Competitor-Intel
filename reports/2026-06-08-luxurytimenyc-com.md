Luxtury Time just published a "Definitive Rolex Submariner Buying Guide 2026 Collector's Edition." Luxtury Time published "Audemars Piguet vs. Patek Philippe Investment: The 2026 Collectors Analysis." Wrist Aficionado does not have an equivalent. This query cluster ("AP vs Patek," "Audemars Piguet vs Patek Philippe," "best watch for investment") sits at the intersection of collector intent and investment content, which is a documented strength for Wrist Aficionado (see `/blogs/news/daily-wear-vs-investment-patek-philippe-aquanaut-travel-time` and `/blogs/news/the-impact-of-u-s-tariffs-on-the-watch-market`). Build a dedicated comparison post. Link out to existing collection pages for both brands.

Luxtury Time published this exact topic this week. Wrist Aficionado has no equivalent in the existing page list. This is a genuine content gap. A well-structured guide targeting "how to start a watch collection," "first luxury watch to buy," and "luxury watch collection guide" would support top-of-funnel acquisition and link naturally to existing brand and model collection pages across the site.

Two of Luxtury Time's new posts target NYC physical-location queries. These are not relevant to Wrist Aficionado's national and online-first model. Do not allocate resources to mirror this content.
