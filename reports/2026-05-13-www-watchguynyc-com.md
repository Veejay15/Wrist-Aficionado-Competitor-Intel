## 5. Recommended Actions for Wrist Aficionado

**1. Strengthen the existing Rolex Datejust category pages to defend against Watch Guy NYC's size-specific page build.**
Wrist Aficionado has `/collections/rolex-datejust` and `/collections/rolex-datejust-two-tone` but does not have dedicated size-split pages (28mm, 31mm, 36mm, 41mm). Watch Guy NYC is ranking at position 4 for "rolex datejust 36mm" (6,600 searches/month) and position 9 for "rolex datejust 41" (19,100 searches/month) off exactly these size pages. Build `/collections/rolex-datejust-36mm` and `/collections/rolex-datejust-41mm` as new collection pages with copy targeting the size-specific buyer intent.

**2. Build a dedicated women's Rolex collection page.**
Wrist Aficionado does not have an equivalent to Watch Guy NYC's `/collections/rolex/ladies` page, which is capturing an estimated 1,612 monthly visits across five keyword variants ("womens rolex," "women's rolex watch," "women's rolex," "rolex ladies," "rolex watch women," "womens rolex watch"). This is a genuine content gap. Create `/collections/womens-rolex-watches` or a filtered equivalent and write introductory copy targeting women's Rolex buying intent.

**3. Capitalize on Watch Guy NYC's "used rolex" ranking collapse.**
Watch Guy NYC dropped from position 2 to position 24 for "used rolex" (18,100 searches/month) this week. Wrist Aficionado has `/collections/pre-owned-luxury-watches` but does not have a page specifically targeting "used rolex" or "pre-owned rolex" intent. Build `/collections/pre-owned-rolex-watches` with copy optimized for "used rolex," "pre-owned rolex," and "second hand rolex" queries, and link to it from the main Rolex collection page. Strike while Watch Guy NYC is vulnerable on this term.

**4. Audit and expand the existing certified pre-owned infrastructure.**
Watch Guy NYC's `/collections/certified-pre-owned-watches` page ranks position 1 for "used luxury watches." Wrist Aficionado has `/collections/pre-owned-luxury-watches`, which covers similar intent but may not be optimized for CPO-specific language. Update that page to incorporate "certified pre-owned," "CPO luxury watches," and related trust signals (authentication process, condition grading, warranty terms). Do not build a new page. Expand the existing one at `/collections/pre-owned-luxury-watches`.

**5. Build price-band collection pages to match Watch Guy NYC's new infrastructure.**
Watch Guy NYC has now indexed four price-band pages (under $5,000, $5,000 to $10,000, $10,000 to $100,000, over $100,000). Wrist Aficionado has `/collections/10-000-to-20-000-luxury-watches`, `/collections/watches-under-10-000`, `/collections/watches-under-20-000`, `/collections/watches-over-50-000`, and `/collections/watches-over-100-000`, but is missing a dedicated $20,000 to $50,000 band and a clear $50,000 to $100,000 band. These price bands capture buyers who search by budget rather than brand. Fill the gap with a `/collections/20-000-to-50-000-luxury-watches` page.

**6. Build an Audemars Piguet New York local landing page.**
Watch Guy NYC updated `/collections/audemars-piguet-new-york` on May 11, and "audemar piguet" ranks at position 7 (5,700 searches/month) pointing to that local page. Wrist Aficionado has `/collections/audemars-piguet-new-york-city` which appears to be equivalent. Review that page and confirm it is optimized for local AP buyer intent in New York, with NAP signals, appointment language, and inventory links. If the page is thin, expand it rather than letting Watch Guy NYC's fresher update hold the local advantage.

**7. Monitor Watch Guy NYC's "rolex authorized dealer" ranking regression.**
Their drop from position 12 to position 20 on "rolex authorized dealer" (6,600 searches/month) is an opening. Wrist Aficionado's `/pages/sell-your-rolex-watch`, `/pages/100-certified-authentic`, and main Rolex collection page should be internally linked to a trust page that directly addresses authorized vs. grey market dealer buying. This is also relevant for Wrist Aficionado's own sell pages and authentication positioning.
