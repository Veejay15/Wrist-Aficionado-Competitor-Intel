Wrist Aficionado already has `/collections/audemars-piguet-royal-oak` and `/collections/audemars-piguet-royal-oak-selfwinding`. Audit both pages immediately: add or expand above-the-fold editorial copy, reinforce internal linking from AP sub-model pages (Royal Oak Jumbo, Chronograph, Offshore, etc.), and ensure schema markup is in place.
