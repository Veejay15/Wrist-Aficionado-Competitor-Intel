## 5. Recommended Actions for Wrist Aficionado

**1. Strengthen the Audemars Piguet Royal Oak collection hub to defend against Bob's "royal oak" surge.**
Bob's moved from position 30 to 6 on "royal oak" (74,000 monthly searches) in one week. Wrist Aficionado already has /collections/audemars-piguet-royal-oak. Prioritize this page immediately: expand the on-page copy with structured content covering the Royal Oak's history, key references, material variants, and why collectors buy pre-owned. Add internal links from all AP sub-collection pages (/collections/audemars-piguet-royal-oak-jumbo, /collections/audemars-piguet-royal-oak-offshore, etc.) to the hub. The goal is to signal to Google that this is a deeper authority page, not just a category shell.

**2. Build a Father's Day watch gift guide to compete with Bob's opensend.com-linked collection.**
Bob's picked up their only high-authority link this week (opensend.com, DR 80) pointing to a Father's Day gift collection. Wrist Aficionado already has /collections/fathers-day-watch-collection. Develop a proper editorial gift guide as a blog post (e.g., /blogs/news/best-fathers-day-watch-gifts-2026) with structured sections by budget, brand, and collector profile. This gives you a linkable, rankable asset for the season and a page worth pitching to gift guide publishers and newsletters, the same editorial context that earned Bob's their best link this week.

**3. Publish a Rolex Milgauss buying guide to contest Bob's position 3 ranking.**
Bob's jumped from position 6 to 3 on "rolex milgauss" (27,100 monthly searches) using their /rolex-milgauss-1.html inventory page. Wrist Aficionado has /collections/rolex-milgauss as a collection page. Complement it with a dedicated editorial post, for example /blogs/news/rolex-milgauss-buyers-guide, covering reference history (116400, 116400GV), the green sapphire crystal, discontinued status, and current market pricing. Wrist Aficionado already has the collection page as a conversion destination; the guide adds the informational entry point to capture earlier-funnel searches.

**4. Expand the Rolex Datejust collection presence in response to Bob's massive Datejust inventory push.**
Bob's added dozens of Datejust-specific product pages this week across virtually every modern reference (126200, 126231, 126233, 126234, 126284, 126300, 126331, 126333). Wrist Aficionado has /collections/rolex-datejust but no dedicated buying guide content for this model family. Consider publishing /blogs/news/rolex-datejust-buyers-guide covering the 36mm vs. 41mm distinction, Jubilee vs. Oyster bracelet options, two-tone vs. steel configurations, and key references to know. This positions Wrist Aficionado to capture informational Datejust queries where Bob's inventory depth alone does not serve buyers doing research.

**5. Target the Cartier category gap created by Bob's ranking drop.**
Bob's fell from position 9 to 18 on "cartier watch" (246,000 monthly searches) this week despite adding Cartier inventory. Wrist Aficionado has /collections/cartier and several Cartier sub-collections (Santos, Tank, Ballon Bleu, etc.). Strengthen the main Cartier hub page with editorial copy, a brief brand overview, key model descriptions, and internal links to each sub-collection. This is a direct opportunity to gain ground on a high-volume term while Bob's category page is weakening.

**6. Pursue a placement on opensend.com or comparable marketing/gifting editorial sites.**
Bob's best link this week came from a gift guide context on opensend.com (domain_inlink_rank 80). Wrist Aficionado's Father's Day collection (/collections/fathers-day-watch-collection) and graduation gift collection (/collections/graduation-watch-gift-guide) are natural targets for this type of outreach. Identify three to five marketing-focused or gifting-adjacent publications running "Father's Day gift for him" editorial pieces in the next two to three weeks and pitch Wrist Aficionado as a source. The blog post recommended in action item 2 will serve as the linkable asset for this outreach.
