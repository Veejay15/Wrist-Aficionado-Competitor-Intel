## 3. Backlink Movements

**Overall profile (as of 2026-08-03):**
Watchguynyc.com carries 114,729 total backlinks from 2,997 referring domains, with a domain inlink rank of 50. The profile is overwhelmingly dofollow: 114,457 dofollow links versus only 272 nofollow, representing a 99.8% dofollow rate, which is unusually clean for a site of this size. The most common anchor patterns by referring domain are blank/image anchors (116 domains), followed by product-specific anchors referencing Audemars Piguet Royal Oak SKUs (73 and 71 domains respectively) and branded anchors such as "watchguynyc.com" (50 domains). The highest-linked pages by backlink count are product image files hosted on the Shopify CDN, indicating that a significant portion of the inbound link equity is directed at image assets rather than indexable content pages. The US accounts for the largest share of referring domains (1,542), followed by Germany (182) and France (95).

No high-authority backlink movements this week (all new links were below domain rank 30).

All four new backlinks detected between July 27 and August 3 originated from low-authority sources: two nofollow links from stealthatfit.com (domain rank 1) pointing to individual Yacht-Master and Submariner product pages, one dofollow link from luxurywatchesworlds.com (domain rank 0) on a Brad Pitt and Patek Philippe Nautilus 5711 article pointing to the Patek Philippe collection page, and one dofollow link from wristwatchfieldguide.com (domain rank 3) on a Panerai PAM00335 reference guide page pointing to a specific Panerai product listing.
