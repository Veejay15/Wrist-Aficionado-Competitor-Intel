## 3. Backlink Movements

**Overall profile (as of 2026-07-06):**

Bob's Watches carries a substantial backlink profile with 815,271 total backlinks across 12,256 referring domains, and a domain inlink rank of 75. The dofollow-to-nofollow ratio is heavily skewed toward dofollow, with 783,461 dofollow links versus 31,810 nofollow links, reflecting a link profile that carries meaningful raw equity on paper. However, the top anchor data reveals a significant profile health concern: multiple high-volume anchor strings are obvious spam injection patterns (e.g., "TELEGRAM @SEO_ANOMALY - SEO BACKLINKS, BLACK-LINKS, TRAFFIC BOOST, LINK INDEXING" appearing across 300 referring domains, and similar Telegram-based anchors). These are consistent with a site that has accumulated manipulative link spam at scale. The legitimate branded anchors, led by "Bobs Watches" (183,764 backlinks) and "Bob's Watches" (349 referring domains), dominate the clean portion of the profile. Edu backlinks number 3,969 across 101 referring domains, and gov backlinks stand at 159 across 17 domains, both likely inflated by the same spam patterns.
