## 1. Executive Summary

- Bob's Watches executed a large-scale sitemap restructure this week, with 1,142 new URLs added and 1,139 removed. The bulk of removals were legacy Rolex product pages using an older URL pattern (e.g., `mens-rolex-*`), replaced by a cleaner, reference-structured URL format. This signals a deliberate site architecture migration rather than a content purge.
- The heaviest new page activity was in Breitling, with over 25 new product listings added, alongside meaningful expansion into Cartier Tank and Must de Cartier variants, Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Offshore and Code 11.59, and a wide sweep of Ladies Rolex Datejust and Oyster Perpetual reference pages.

The majority of new links originated from a low-authority watch pricing aggregator (360d.io) and a bulk asset-tracking site (assetaddicts.com), both with domain rank of 1.
