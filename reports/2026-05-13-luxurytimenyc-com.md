## 5. Recommended Actions for Wrist Aficionado

**1. Launch a city-specific "buy luxury watches in [city]" page program, starting with the highest-value markets.**

Luxtury Time just built 30 of these pages targeting Rolex specifically. Wrist Aficionado has no equivalent geo-targeted landing pages. Given that Wrist Aficionado carries Rolex, AP, Patek, and Richard Mille, the opportunity is broader. Build pages for New York, Miami, Los Angeles, Dallas, and Houston first (those are the highest overlap between luxury watch buyer populations and Luxtury Time's new page set). Target the pattern "buy [brand] watches in [city]" and cover Rolex, Patek Philippe, and Audemars Piguet in each city. Each page should include current inventory, trust signals, and a concierge CTA.

**2. Strengthen the existing /collections/audemars-piguet-royal-oak page to compete on "royal oak" (74,000 volume, position 3 for Luxtury Time).**

Wrist Aficionado already has /collections/audemars-piguet-royal-oak. Do not build a new page. Instead, audit the existing page this week. Add a substantive editorial header section covering the Royal Oak's collectibility, key references, and current market pricing. This page needs to be a stronger commercial landing page to contest position 3 on a 74,000-volume keyword. Cross-link from the Royal Oak Jumbo, Offshore, and Chronograph collection pages.

**3. Build out an authentication and trust page that directly competes with Luxtury Time's new /pages/authentication-guarantee.**

Wrist Aficionado has /pages/100-certified-authentic but that slug reads more like a marketing label than a functional trust page. Create a standalone, substantive authentication page that covers the inspection process, certifications, box and papers policy, and buyer protections in plain language. Target queries like "how to authenticate a luxury watch" and "certified pre-owned rolex." This is a conversion-critical page category that Luxtury Time is now competing in directly.

**4. Build a "sell or trade your watch" page that competes with Luxtury Time's /pages/sell-or-trade.**

Wrist Aficionado has /pages/sell-your-luxury-watch and multiple brand-specific sell pages (sell your Rolex, sell your Patek, etc.). These are already strong assets. No new build is needed. However, consolidate internal linking so that these pages are accessible from the main navigation and from relevant collection pages. Luxtury Time's new sell-or-trade page suggests they are pushing harder into the consignment and trade-in funnel.

**5. Target "rolex datejust 41" and "datejust 41" directly, as Luxtury Time holds position 2 to 3 on 25,000 combined monthly searches.**

Wrist Aficionado has /collections/rolex-datejust. Check whether this page is specifically optimized for the 41mm variant. If the 41mm is not broken out as a filterable or linked sub-collection, add a dedicated section or anchor targeting "Rolex Datejust 41" with current inventory and a short model guide. Do not build a new standalone page unless the existing Datejust collection page cannot be adapted, as that is the natural home for this content.

**6. Build a "luxury watches for men" collection page to compete with Luxtury Time's /collections/luxury-watches-for-men, which is now ranking at position 14 on a 33,100-volume keyword after jumping 7 positions this week.**

Wrist Aficionado has /collections/mens-luxury-watches and /collections/high-end-mens-watches and /collections/expensive-watches-for-men. Do not build a new page. Instead, determine which of these three pages is currently performing best in search and consolidate SEO equity by canonicalizing or redirecting the weaker two to the strongest one. Three competing pages splitting the same intent is likely hurting performance. Pick one URL, optimize it fully, and redirect or canonicalize the others.

**7. Monitor Luxtury Time's city-specific pages for ranking traction in 30 to 60 days.**

These pages were just indexed. They will not rank immediately. Set a tracking alert for the queries "buy rolex watches in [miami/los angeles/dallas]" and check Luxtury Time's position in four weeks. If they begin ranking in positions 1 to 10, accelerate the city page build described in recommendation 1. If they do not rank, assess the page quality before committing to the same format.
